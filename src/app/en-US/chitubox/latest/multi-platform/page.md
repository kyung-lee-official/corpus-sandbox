# Multi-Platform

It's quite common that sometimes your model is too large for your build plate and you will have to separate it into smaller parts and print it in multiple times. Multi-platform allows you to place your model parts on different virtual build plates, slice and save all of them as individual sliced files at once, making the printing process more efficient.

## Platform Quick Access Tools

-   **Edit Platform Name**: Modify the name of the corresponding platform.
-   **Orient**: Adjust the orientation of the models in the corresponding platform.
-   **Layout**: Arrange the models on the corresponding platform.
-   **Copy**: Duplicate the models in the corresponding platform to a new platform.
-   **Lock**: Prevent the models in the corresponding platform from being moved or edited.
-   **Show X-Axis & Y-Axis Slider**: Display the sliders for the corresponding platform, this is useful for checking internal details of the model.
