# ChituHive

ChituHive is a collaborative platform that connects 3D model creators with the CHITUBOX community. Creators can share their designs, whether free or paid, while users can easily browse, download, and print a diverse collection of models directly within CHITUBOX.

More specifically, ChituHive offers the following services:

### Model Library

Access a comprehensive collection of high-quality 3D models from both trusted third-party platforms and original designs from creators who collaborate directly with CHITUBOX. Whether you're looking for artistic pieces, functional parts, or unique designs, you'll find models suited for every project.

### Print-to-Door Service

Take the hassle out of 3D printing with our print-to-door service. Simply select your favorite models, choose your preferred colors and finishes, and we'll handle the printing and delivery—bringing professionally finished models straight to your doorstep.

### AI-Powered Creation Tools

Unleash your creativity with our advanced AI-powered modeling tools. Generate unique 3D models from text descriptions or images, then publish your creations to the model library to earn revenue or process them directly within CHITUBOX for immediate use.

### Standard Parts Library

Streamline your workflow with our built-in standard parts library, featuring commonly used components and mechanical parts that integrate seamlessly into your design process within CHITUBOX.
