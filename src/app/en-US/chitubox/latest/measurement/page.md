# Measurement

CHITUBOX provides a variety of measurement tools to help you accurately measure your 3D models. These tools include:

-   **Two Point Distance**: Measure the distance between arbitrary points on your model.
-   **Point to Surface Distance**: Measure the distance between a point and the surface of your model. Note that this measurement is always perpendicular to the surface.
-   **Parallel Surface Distance**: Measure the distance between two parallel surfaces on your model.
