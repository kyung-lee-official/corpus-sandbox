# ChituAction

ChituAction is a powerful feature in CHITUBOX that allows you to automate various tasks and streamline your workflow. With ChituAction, you can create custom actions to enhance your 3D printing experience.

ChituAction entry  is located at the upper-left of the Main view.

The standard workflow provided in ChituAction includes the following steps in sequence: Scale, Orient, Hollow, Drill, Supports, Layout, Export Model, Save Project, and Save Slice. However, you can customize this workflow by dragging either end to exclude steps that aren't needed. For more precise control, click the Settings button  to toggle individual steps on or off as required.

You can preset up to **three Processes** in **ChituAction**, allowing you to configure different parameter settings in advance and switch between them quickly as needed.
