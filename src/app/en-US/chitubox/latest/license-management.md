# License Management

## My Account

Please click here and sign up if you have not registered a CHITUBOX account.

## My Subscription

You could check your subscription plans and valid days here.

    ![](../../../../../public/images/docs/en-US/chitubox/1.x.x/my-subscription.png)

An account can have multiple license keys. The license key will be automatically linked to your account when you make a purchase. You no longer need to activate a license key. Simply log in to your account to access the features included in your subscription plan（Advanced or Pro）.

## Orders & Invoice

You could check your orders and get invoices here.

    ![](../../../../../public/images/docs/en-US/chitubox/1.x.x/orders-and-invoice.png)

## Renewal Management

You could choose whether to renew your license key automatically here.

    ![](../../../../../public/images/docs/en-US/chitubox/1.x.x/renewal-management.png)

## Key Exchange

1. Go to **User Center** and then click **Key Exchange** on the left sidebar.

    ![](../../../../../public/images/docs/en-US/chitubox/1.x.x/license-management-license-card.png)

1. Enter your license key and click on the **Exchange** button to activate the license key and bind it to your computer. The effective period of the key starts from the day the key is activated.

## Device Management

You could manage your devices bound to your license key here.

A personal version of license key can be used on up to two computers. A team version of the license key can be used on up to 5 computers. You can log onto user center anytime to unlink your computers.

    ![](../../../../../public/images/docs/en-US/chitubox/1.x.x/device-management.png)
