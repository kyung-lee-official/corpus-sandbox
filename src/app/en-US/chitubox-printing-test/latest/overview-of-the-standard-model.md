	Parameter information for the CHITUBOX TMC V1 model is detailed around the corresponding model.

				![](../../../../../public/images/docs/en-US/chitubox-printing-test/1.x.x/test-preparation/tma-v3_002.png)

			01. Main Base Plate

			02. Uniform Lattice

			Serial number
			Parameters
			Parameter description
			Effect description

			01
			Main Base Plate

				Assists in judging the deformation resistance of the printed model

			02

			Gradient Supports
			CHITUBOX software: heavy, medium, fine supports

				Assists in judging the extreme support parameters that ensure successful printing in suspended areas (coverage area corresponds to the contact point spacing parameters of the CHITUBOX software)

			04
			Gradient Hole and Shaft Fit

					Cylinder diameter: φ6; Hole radius deviation: +0.05mm, +0.025mm, 0, -0.025mm, -0.05mm

			Assists in judging the optimal hole and shaft fit clearance

			05
			Gradient Wall Thickness
			Wall thicknesses: 0.5mm, 0.4mm, 0.3mm, 0.2mm

				Assists in judging the printing effect of different wall thickness structures

			06
			Gradient Wall Thickness

				Wall thicknesses: 0.5mm, 0.4mm, 0.3mm, 0.2mm

				Assists in judging the printing effect of different wall thickness structures

			07
			Klein Bottle
			Continuous gradient curvature model

			08
			Gradient Hole and Shaft

				Gradient hole and shaft sizes: φ0.025, φ0.5, φ1, φ3, φ5, φ10

				Assists in judging the printability and dimensional accuracy of different types of hole and shaft structures

			09
			Gradient Inclined Cylinder

				Gradient inclination angles: 0°, 15°, 30°, 45°

					Assists in judging the layer transition effect of different cutting angles of curved structures; 

					Assists in judging the sliding effect of the bottom valve structure during the transition of large and small cross-sections between layers

			10
			CHITUBOX Icon

				CHITUBOX logo element, as a copyright marker

			/

			11
			Gradient Groove

				Groove sizes: 0.1mm, 0.2mm, 0.3mm, 0.4mm, 0.5mm, 0.6mm, 0.7mm, 0.8mm

				Assists in judging the printing ability of holes, shafts, bosses, and other detailed structures

			12
			Gradient Steps

				Gradient step structure: 1mm, 2mm, 3mm, 4mm, 5mm, 6mm, 7mm, 8mm

				Assists in judging the printing effect of different cross-wall structures
